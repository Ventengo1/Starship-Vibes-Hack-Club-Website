THE STARSHIP SYSTEM

A fully reusable transportation system designed to carry crew and cargo to Earth orbit, the Moon, Mars, and beyond.

Starship (Ship 42)

The upper stage spacecraft featuring an integrated payload section, 6 Raptor 3 engines, and a thermal protection heat shield built for orbital reentry and tower-arm recovery.

Super Heavy (Booster 22)

The Block 3 first-stage booster powered by 33 Raptor 3 engines, generating over 18 million pounds of thrust at liftoff to propel Starship out of Earth's atmosphere.

FLIGHT 15: HISTORIC MILESTONES

Launching from Orbital Launch Pad 2 at Starbase, Texas, Flight 15 changed orbital spaceflight forever.

Raptor 3 & Propellant

CH4 + LOX

Full flow staged combustion running methalox across 33 booster engines and 6 ship engines, stripping out external heat shields and tons of extra plumbing so more mass goes to orbit or back onto the tower.

  • Chamber pressure north of 350 bar
  • Integrated regenerative cooling channels
  • Cleaner engine bay after reentry heat

Mission Outcome

Ship 42 Success: After completing 14 orbits at an altitude of ~276 km and deploying its full payload of 26 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites, Ship 42 executed a controlled deorbit burn, survived fiery atmospheric reentry, and performed a precision flip into Mechazilla's catch arms at Starbase on November 14, 2026.

Booster 22 Diversion: Following a nominal ascent and hot-staging separation, Booster 22 experienced an early boostback engine shutdown and low-fuel alert, autonomously diverting away from the launch tower for a safe offshore splashdown in the Gulf.
